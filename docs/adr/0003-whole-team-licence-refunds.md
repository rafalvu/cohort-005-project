# Refund whole team licences

Extend refund review to whole team licences requested by the purchasing team admin. Approval invalidates unused seat codes and revokes access granted by redeemed seats, retaining affected learners’ progress as archived history. Access from a separate purchase remains valid. This supersedes the exclusion of team purchases in [ADR 0002](0002-refund-approval-without-payment-processing.md); partial refunds and real payment processing remain outside the flow.
