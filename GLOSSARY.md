# Course refunds

The language used for refunds of course purchases.

## Language

**Refund**:
Money returned to a learner for a course purchase.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, reversal, purchase cancellation

**Refund request**:
A learner’s request for a refund, subject to review. A pending refund request is awaiting a decision and does not mean money has been returned.
_Avoid_: Chargeback request, reversal request, cancellation request
