# Course refunds

The language used for refunds of course purchases.

## Language

**Refund**:
Money returned to the purchaser for a course purchase.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, reversal, purchase cancellation

**Refund request**:
A learner’s or purchasing team admin’s request for a refund, subject to review. A pending refund request is awaiting a decision and does not mean money has been returned.
_Avoid_: Chargeback request, reversal request, cancellation request

**Active progress**:
A learner’s lesson completion, quiz attempts, and video progress for their current course access.

**Archived progress**:
Learning history retained after refund approval, separate from the learner’s active progress. It does not carry over when the learner purchases the course again.

**Refund approval**:
An admin’s decision to accept a refund request for the full original amount paid. Approval ends the learner’s course access and archives their progress, but does not confirm that money has been returned.

**Refund decline**:
An admin’s decision to reject a refund request, leaving the learner’s course access and progress unchanged.

**Team licence**:
A set of seats for one course bought together in a single team purchase.

**Seat**:
One learner’s right to access a course through a team licence. An unused seat can be redeemed by a learner.

**Team refund request**:
A purchasing team admin’s request to refund an entire team licence, covering all its seats rather than selected seats.
