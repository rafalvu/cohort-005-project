# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary defines the business terms used for course access, purchases, and refunds.

## Language

### People

**Platform Admin**:
A member of Cadence staff responsible for running the platform and deciding Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), the team, Staff

**Team Admin**:
A learner who buys and manages a Team Licence.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own)

**Instructor**:
A person who creates and manages their own courses and sees the learners enrolled on them.
_Avoid_: Teacher, Author, Creator

### Purchases

**Purchase**:
A single payment for a course, either an Individual Purchase or a Team Licence.
_Avoid_: Order, Transaction

**Individual Purchase**:
A Purchase that gives the buyer access to a course for themselves.

**Team Licence**:
A Purchase that grants Seats for other learners rather than course access for the buyer.
_Avoid_: Team purchase, Bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One place on a Team Licence that a learner can claim for course access. A Seat is unclaimed until a learner claims it, then belongs to that learner.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Code, Voucher

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
The buyer's request, with a reason, to get their money back for a specific Purchase. It is open while awaiting a Platform Admin's decision, then approved or declined.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the request itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback, Pending (for an open request)

**Refund**:
Money to be returned to the buyer after a Refund Request is approved. A Refund is pending until the transfer is confirmed, issued when confirmed as sent, or failed if the money could not be sent.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

### Course access

**Lapsed**:
A learner's course access that is no longer active.

**Watched**:
The furthest video position reached in each lesson, added up across a course and measured in seconds.
_Avoid_: Viewed, Progress
